01. Our Mission

"To build AI systems that meaningfully connect mathematics, computation, and health — systems that are not only powerful, but explainable, reliable, and socially responsible."

— MAIRI Core Mandate
⬡

Mathematical Rigor

We ground every AI system in sound mathematical theory — ODE-based modeling, topological structures — ensuring stability and interpretability.

⬡

Clinical Relevance

Research is only meaningful if it helps patients. Every MAIRI project is evaluated against its real-world clinical impact potential.

⬡

Responsible AI

We are committed to building systems that are fair, transparent, and accountable — especially in high-stakes healthcare environments.

⬡

Open Collaboration

Science advances faster together. We actively seek partnerships with researchers, clinicians, and institutions worldwide.

02. Research Areas

01

Clinical AI & Healthcare Analytics

AI systems for medical risk prediction, clinical decision support, and patient outcome modeling using real-world healthcare datasets including MIMIC-IV.

Risk PredictionClinical NLPMIMIC-IV
02

Mathematical Foundations of ML

ODE-driven modeling, multiset topology, and combinatorial structures to understand learning dynamics and build interpretable, stable models.

ODE ModelingTopologyFuzzy Systems
03

Model Optimization & Compression

Graph-theoretic approaches to neural network pruning. Eigenvector centrality–based methods for efficient fine-tuning of large-scale CNN architectures.

CNN PruningGraph TheoryVGG / ResNet
04

Quantum-Enhanced Biosensing

AI-integrated quantum-enhanced fiber-optic biosensors for real-time non-invasive diagnostics, leveraging quantum metrology and ML fusion.

Quantum MLBiosensorsQKD
05

NLP & Clinical Document AI

Transformer-based approaches for medical NLP, clinical document understanding, and AI-generated content detection in healthcare systems.

BERTTransformersMedical NLP
06

Neuroscience & Brain-AI Interface

Computational models of neural systems and AI-driven approaches to understanding brain function, with the Neuroscience Foundation.

Neuro AIBrain ModelingSignals
